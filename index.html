Gender and ChatGPT in Norway

Replications and analyses of the open data behind kiindeksen.no/kjonn.html ("Menn, kvinner og ChatGPT"). Each analysis is a script that reads the data files and can be rerun.

Independent analysis of open data. Not an official KI-indeksen publication.

    Replication of the headline numbers

  2. 02 · 9 November 2026

    The rise in women's share, with a fixed topic mix

    At most 3.63 points from the topic mix, across the tests

    With the topic mix held fixed, four topics give a rise of 10.5 points, against 10.4 in the published total. The page's files do not include the monthly mix, so the mix effect is what remains. Analysis 03 measures it, and the conclusion holds.

  3. 03 · 9 November 2026

    The rise in women's share, with the measured topic mix

    +10.56 points within topics, of a 10.43-point rise

    With OpenAI's monthly topic mix, the rise splits into +10.56 points within topics, −0.74 from the mix and +0.61 not explained. Writing's share of messages fell by about half, but other shifts largely offset that. This is consistent with women catching up on individual ChatGPT accounts. The data does not cover AI use overall.
